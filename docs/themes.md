# DependIQ Themes

DependIQ now offers 7 beautiful themes to customize your experience!

## Available Themes

### Light Theme
**Perfect for:** Daytime use, well-lit environments

A clean, bright interface with:
- Background: `#fafafa` / `#ffffff`
- High contrast for readability
- Professional appearance

### Dark Themes

#### Classic Dark
**Perfect for:** Night-time coding, reducing eye strain

**Perfect for:** Long coding sessions, calming atmosphere

Deep blue tones inspired by the ocean:

**Perfect for:** Natural feel, reducing eye strain

Calming green tones inspired by nature:
- Background: `#1a2f1a` → `#2d4a2d`
- Accent: `#66bb6a`

- Unique and refreshing

### Developer Favorites

#### Nord 🏔️
**Perfect for:** Fans of popular IDE themes

Arctic-inspired color scheme:
- Background: `#2e3440` → `#3b4252`

### Auto Theme

#### System
**Perfect for:** Seamless OS integration

Automatically matches your operating system's theme preference:
- Switches between light and dark based on OS setting
- Updates automatically when you change OS theme
- Zero manual switching needed
- Perfect for users who change themes throughout the day

## How to Change Themes

1. Click on your profile icon in the sidebar
2. Navigate to the **Preferences** section
3. Browse through the available themes
4. Click on any theme to apply it instantly
5. Your preference is saved automatically

## Theme Features

### Instant Application
- Themes apply immediately when selected
- No page reload required
- Smooth transition animations

### Consistent Experience
- All themes maintain:
  - Clear status colors (success = green, error = red, warning = yellow)
  - Readable text across all UI elements
  - Proper contrast ratios for accessibility

### Persistent Across Sessions
- Your theme choice is saved to your account
- Automatically applied on all devices
- Syncs across all pages instantly

### System Theme Detection
- The "System" theme automatically detects your OS preference
- Updates in real-time when you change your OS theme
- No manual intervention needed

## Accessibility

All themes meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards for:
- Color contrast ratios
- Text readability
- Interactive element visibility

## Tips

1. **For Long Sessions**: Try Ocean, Forest, or Nord themes - designed to reduce eye strain
2. **For Maximum Focus**: Classic Dark provides minimal distraction
3. **For Variety**: System theme changes automatically with your OS
4. **For Personality**: Dracula adds vibrant colors to your workspace
